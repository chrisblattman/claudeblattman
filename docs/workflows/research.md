# Research

Before starting a project or deciding what to do next, I often want to know what other people have already tried. AI can help search for that work, compare the evidence, and find gaps in what we know.

There are two ways I do this:

| Approach | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **[Multi-agent research](deep-research.md#multi-agent-research)** | Use `/research-council` to have agents search independently, check their sources, and bring their findings together. |
| **[Browser Deep Research](deep-research.md#browser-deep-research)** | Use `/deep-research` to commission reports from ChatGPT and Gemini's dedicated Deep Research tools. |

Both links lead into the same [research guide](deep-research.md), which explains the choices, setup, and how to ask a useful question. Choose the route that fits the work; you don't need to run both.

Save the findings with the project they inform. The [project-management guides](projects-and-research.md) explain how to keep that evidence alongside the project's description, decisions, and next steps.
