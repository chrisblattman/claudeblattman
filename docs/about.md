# About Chris' team

## About Chris Blattman

I'm a political economist at the [Harris School of Public Policy](https://harris.uchicago.edu/) at the University of Chicago. My research focuses on violence, crime, and poverty. I design and test interventions in cities across Latin America and Africa.

- I write about research, conflict, policy, and increasingly about AI tools on [X/Twitter (@cblatts)](https://twitter.com/cblatts) and at [chrisblattman.com](https://chrisblattman.com/).
- I've written a book about the causes of war and peace: [*Why We Fight*](https://chrisblattman.com/why-we-fight/).
- For over a decade I've written practical advice on [my blog](https://chrisblattman.com/) for [students](https://chrisblattman.com/undergrad-ma/), [PhD researchers](https://chrisblattman.com/phd-advice/), and [faculty](https://chrisblattman.com/advice-professors/), including [how to write well](https://chrisblattman.com/writing/).

## The team behind this site

These guides grow out of the AI workflows I use for research, administration, and everyday life. **Shay Houseknecht** worked with me on the October 2026 site refresh, turning those workflows into practical walkthroughs and reworking the site's content and organization.

We build and revise the site with help from AI tools. My research assistants also help maintain and improve the workflows. The site draws on others who share their approaches publicly; you'll find those influences on the [Resources](resources.md) page.

## About the project

“Claude Blattman” is my name for this evolving setup. I started building it in January 2026 to get more of the help I wanted from an executive assistant, a project manager, and a research collaborator. [My AI principles](how-i-use-ai.md) explains how I use it and what I've learned.

We share the guides, skills, and templates freely so you can adapt them to your own work. The [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/chrisblattman/claudeblattman), under the MIT License. The site is built with [Material for MkDocs](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/) and hosted on GitHub Pages.
