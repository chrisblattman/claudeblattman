# Email review and continuation

## Scope and discovery

Bare check-in starts a new last-24-hours review. Catch-up defaults to 14 days. Resume reconciles a known batch. Continuation retains its original scope and explicit exclusions. An explicit fixed end is fixed; otherwise record an initial cutoff and one final refresh after substantive preparation. Overlap time boundaries and deduplicate IDs.

Main discovery uses Inbox mail, both read and unread. Include configured family/quarantine sources and known particular unfinished commitments, without treating them as permission for an all-mailbox audit. Already-filed announcements are not ordinary review filler. A message bearing both INBOX and an announcements label remains eligible. A self-sent report can be relevant; the SENT label alone does not disqualify it.

Follow all result pages within the stated scope. Fetch complete relevant bodies/HTML and attachments only where needed for a decision. Preserve timestamp discrepancies as uncertainty rather than discarding a possibly new arrival. Separate reviewed choices from metadata-only candidates and failed reads. Keep useful unshown candidates from recent batches, subject to current labels and scope. Previously numbered pending items keep their original batch/item reference instead of becoming fresh numbered tasks.

If broader_folder_recovery is enabled with defined allowed folders and permission, inspect those folders for substantive overlooked obligations. Verify handling first. Restore exact qualifying IDs to Inbox only under that permission, preserving read state. Report failed recovery without hiding the item. Do not impose broader recovery merely because the host is Claude rather than Codex.

## Check that work is still open

Before recommending a reply/action, identify the actual request, owner, or evidenced commitment. Inspect the current thread through the presentation cutoff. If a separate reply or forward could have resolved it, make a focused Sent search using actual addresses and distinctive content. No matching result in a narrow search is not proof of no reply.

Match completion to the obligation: an attachment resend needs attachment delivery evidence, an approved draft is not delivery, and a holding reply leaves the promised task open. A later bounce or renewed ask can reopen it. Delegation means waiting on that owner unless the user has a separate commitment. Read/unread state and an old remaining-work note do not establish current obligations.

Record a compact handling check with source/thread/Sent evidence IDs, checked time, completeness, disposition, owner, and actual remaining work. Mark unresolved evidence “status unverified” instead of claiming unanswered or complete. Do not audit all Sent mail or delay a useful review to investigate an unrelated research question.

## Presentation and exact source targets

Before FYI read updates or accepting numeric decisions, persist the exact batch mapping with the bundled writer and read it back. Use at most ten FYIs followed by ten attention items, numbered continuously from 1. These are display limits, not search limits. Hold references and previously numbered pending tasks remain identifiable by topic and original batch/item.

FYI auto-marking requires specific setup permission, fully reviewed content/context, no remaining task/hold, and exact message-level mutation plus readback. It removes only UNREAD. Do not apply a whole-thread operation that would include unreviewed newer messages. Preserve INBOX and other labels. If exact-ID updates are unavailable, leave the source unchanged and disclose that limitation. Failed/unknown changes remain pending, not falsely reported as marked read.

An unopened document with unknown implications or intentional later reading is Needs attention even when no reply is needed. Already-read awareness usually does not consume FYI slots; the configured family update exception can surface a new important personal update as already read.

At the end of a displayed batch, briefly give coverage/remainder and enabled extra-review status. Name useful unshown choices, distinguish reviewed from found-but-unreviewed, and disclose gaps. Do not derive useful remainder counts by subtracting displayed items from unread totals. Do not repeat the offer for more while waiting for approval of a draft.

During mixed dictated instructions, apply each clear action to its original targets. New messages do not inherit old approvals. Resolve ambiguous numbers when another batch or subject is plausible. Preserve unmentioned items, holds, drafts, and partial success. Recheck task status at final freshness even when no new inbox mail arrived.
